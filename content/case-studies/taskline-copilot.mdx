## What's coming

TaskLine started as a full-stack workspace app — Kanban, Calendar and Timeline views on a NestJS + Prisma + PostgreSQL monorepo. The Copilot phase re-asks its core question: what should planning feel like when the software can think? Natural-language capture ("sometime next week, prep for the exam, takes ~2h"), AI breakdowns that show a plan preview _before_ touching your board, confidence you can see, and undo you can trust.

The full case study — decisions, trade-offs and what breaks — lands when v2 ships. Version 1 is live at taskline-elya.vercel.app.
