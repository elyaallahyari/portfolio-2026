## What's coming

Since June 2025 the European Accessibility Act makes WCAG 2.1 AA a legal requirement, not a nice-to-have — and most teams still find out from a lawsuit-shaped email. A11y Copilot scans a live site for real failure modes (keyboard traps, unlabeled inputs, contrast, missing alt text), explains each one in plain language with the exact WCAG success criterion cited, and proposes a fix — which a human reviews and approves before anything changes.

Accessibility + AI + human review is also the direction I want for my master's thesis, which is why this project exists. The full case study lands with the first working scan.
